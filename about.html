About Northlane

We started Northlane because most consulting engagements end at the recommendation.

Founded in 2016, we work alongside operating teams until a fix is actually running — not just approved in a boardroom.

Our starting point

Most growth problems aren't strategy problems — they're operating problems wearing a strategy costume. A sales team hitting its number but drowning fulfillment. A new market entry that assumes capacity the business doesn't have. We start by finding the real constraint, then build the plan around it.

How we work

Small teams, direct access to decision-makers, and a bias toward implementation. We typically embed two to three consultants on-site for the diagnostic phase, then stay through the first cycle of change so we're accountable to the result, not just the report.
