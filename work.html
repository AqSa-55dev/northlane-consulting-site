Case study

Cascade Materials

Cascade's fulfillment process had grown by exception for a decade — eleven decision points across four systems, none of them documented the same way twice. Orders were accurate but slow, and nobody could say exactly why.

We spent three weeks mapping the process floor-to-system, then rebuilt it around three decision points instead of eleven, with clear ownership at each one. Dispatch and warehouse leads co-designed the new flow rather than receiving it.

Results at 6 months
