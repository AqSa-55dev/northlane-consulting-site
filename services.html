In detail

Operations Diagnostic

We map the actual flow of work — orders, approvals, handoffs — against where time and money are lost. You leave with a prioritized list of fixes ranked by effort and impact, not a 60-page audit.

  • Process mapping across the affected function
  • Interviews with the people doing the work, not just managing it
  • A fix list ranked by effort and impact

Growth & Market Entry

Before we size a market, we size the organization's ability to serve it. That means honest capacity modeling alongside the demand-side analysis most firms stop at.

  • Demand sizing and competitive positioning
  • Internal capacity and capability assessment
  • A phased entry plan tied to funding stages

Change Management

New systems and structures fail when the people running them weren't part of building them. We embed with the team through the first full operating cycle.

  • Stakeholder mapping and communication plan
  • Training and documentation built with the team, not for them
  • Adoption tracking through the first quarter

Financial Advisory

For companies scaling past founder-led finance — cash flow modeling, cost structure reviews, and reporting that a board can actually act on.

  • 13-week cash flow modeling
  • Cost structure and margin review by line
  • Board-ready monthly reporting templates
